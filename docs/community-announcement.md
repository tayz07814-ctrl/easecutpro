Wanted to give you first look at something the team's been building:

transcript instead of a traditional timeline.

**What it does:**
- 🎙️ Automatic, fully offline transcription with word-level timestamps
- ✂️ Edit by editing text — select words or sentences in the transcript and
  delete them to cut the video, Descript-style
- 🔇 One-click silence removal — detect pauses and shorten or remove them

  magnet/snap and manual drag
- ⬆️ Export straight to MP4

Because it runs locally, your footage never leaves your machine — no
uploads, no cloud processing, no per-minute transcription fees.
