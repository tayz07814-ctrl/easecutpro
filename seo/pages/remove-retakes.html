What counts as a retake

  • Full restarts — "So today we're gonna talk about—" … "So today, we're going to talk about editing."
  • False starts — the two words you got out before stopping.
  • Rephrased repeats — same idea, different words on the second attempt.
  • Production chatter between takes — "hold on, let me fix my light."

Frequently asked questions

How does retake detection work?

Easecut transcribes your footage and compares lines against each other. When you restart a sentence — same words, second attempt — it recognizes the repeat, groups the attempts, and suggests keeping the best take while cutting the rest.

Which take does it keep?

The suggestion favors the complete, clean delivery — usually your final attempt. You see every take side by side in the review, so if you prefer a different one, keep that instead with one click.

Does it work if my retakes aren't word-for-word identical?

Yes. Retakes rarely repeat exactly — you rephrase, trail off, or restart mid-sentence. The detection matches meaningfully repeated lines, false starts, and partial restarts, not just identical strings.

Can I record without a clapper or marker words?

Yes. Just talk, flub, and repeat the line — no need to snap, say a marker word, or leave long gaps. The transcript analysis finds the retakes on its own.
