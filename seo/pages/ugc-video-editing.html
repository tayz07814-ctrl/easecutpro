UGC video editing, at the speed brands expect

UGC economics are brutal: the fee is fixed, so every extra hour of editing comes out of your rate. The recording takes twenty minutes — it's the cleanup that eats the evening. Built for the way UGC is actually shot

A UGC take is never clean. You restart the hook five times to nail the energy, check the brief mid-take, fix your light, and stumble through the CTA twice. That's fine — that's the process. Easecut is tuned for exactly this footage: its retake detection recognizes each restarted line and keeps your best delivery, the silence cutter tightens the pacing for feed-ready energy, and production chatter ("hold on, let me fix my light") never reaches the brand.

One brief, one batch

Most briefs want variations — three hooks, two CTAs, a couple of aspect-ratio-friendly retells. With batch processing you queue every take from the shoot and review them back to back. What used to be a full editing day per brief becomes a single review session.
