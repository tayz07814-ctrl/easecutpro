AI auto-cut: raw footage in, postable video out

Most of editing a talking-head video is deleting things: the retake where you fumbled the line, the pause while you checked your notes, the "wait, did I say that right?" Easecut's AI auto-cut finds all of it — silences, retakes, false starts, filler, production chatter — and queues every cut for a one-glance review before export.

What the AI cuts for you

  • Silences and dead air — the smart silence cutter detects long pauses and collapses them to the pacing you set.
  • Retakes and false starts — when you restart a line, Easecut recognizes the repeated attempts and keeps only the best take.
  • Filler and fumbles — the "um, they're like…" moments that pad your runtime.
  • Production chatter — "hold on, let me fix my light" never makes the final cut.

Auto-cut without losing control

Fully automatic editors are fast until they cut the wrong thing — a pause you wanted for effect, a take you actually liked. Easecut is review-first: every suggested cut is shown with its exact moment and transcript line, and nothing happens to your video until you approve it. It's the speed of automation with the judgment of a human editor — yours.

How it works

  1. Upload your raw recording.
  2. The AI transcribes and scans every word and pause.
  3. You review the suggested cuts and approve the ones you want.
  4. Export a publish-ready video — or an edit list for your NLE.

Frequently asked questions

How does AI auto-cut work?

Easecut transcribes your footage and analyzes both the audio and the transcript. Voice-activity detection finds silences and dead air; the transcript analysis finds retakes, false starts, repeated lines, filler, and production chatter. Everything is flagged as a suggested cut for you to review.

Will it remove silence from my video automatically?

Yes. The smart silence cutter detects long pauses and dead air and collapses them to the pacing you choose, so the result feels tight without sounding chopped.

Can the AI make a cut I don't want?

No — nothing is applied until you approve it. Every suggestion shows the exact moment and transcript line, and you accept or reject each one before export.
