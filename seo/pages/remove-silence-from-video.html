Remove silence from video, automatically

Dead air is the slowest thing to edit by hand: you scrub the waveform, find each gap, razor it out, and repeat fifty times per video. Easecut's silence remover does the whole pass in one shot — voice-activity detection finds every pause and dead-air gap, collapses them to the pacing you choose, and shows you each cut for approval before export.

How silence removal works in Easecut

  1. Upload your video — raw talking-head or podcast-style footage works best.
  2. The AI scans the audio — voice-activity detection separates speech from silence with far more nuance than a simple volume threshold, so breaths and room tone don't fool it.
  3. Pick your pacing — presets range from a natural rhythm that keeps conversational pauses, to tight, fast-cut pacing for short-form. A manual mode unlocks every parameter if you want to hand-tune.
  4. Review and export — every suggested cut is listed with its exact moment. Approve, reject, export.

Why not just use a volume threshold?

Most basic silence cutters mark anything under a volume level as "silence." That misfires constantly: breaths get kept as speech, quiet talking gets cut as silence, and cut edges land mid-word. Easecut uses a speech-detection model plus edge refinement that walks each cut to where the voice actually stops — not just where the noise does — so sentence endings survive and the next line's first syllable is never clipped.

More than a silence remover

Silence is only one kind of dead weight in raw footage. In the same pass, Easecut also flags retakes and false starts, filler words, repeated lines, and production chatter — so one review covers everything, not just the pauses.
