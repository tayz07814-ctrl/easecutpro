The AI video editor for YouTube talking-head content

Most "AI video editors" want to be a whole studio. Easecut does the one thing that eats most of a YouTuber's editing time: turning a raw talking-head recording into a clean cut. The first pass, automated

Editing a talking-head video has two very different halves. The first pass — cutting mistakes, pauses, and flubs — is mechanical, slow, and where most of the hours go. The second — b-roll, graphics, color — is the creative part. Easecut automates the first half completely: retake detection keeps your best takes, the silence cutter sets your pacing, and filler removal sharpens the delivery. What reaches your creative pass is already tight.

  • Commentary, education, product videos — natural pacing presets keep it conversational; plans support videos up to 10 minutes.

Works with your existing workflow

Easecut is transcript-based — your footage becomes text, and the review shows every suggested cut with its transcript line, so auditing an edit takes seconds instead of scrubbing. Export a finished video for straight-to-upload workflows, or an edit list into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or Final Cut when the video needs a full finishing pass.
